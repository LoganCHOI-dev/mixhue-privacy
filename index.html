Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

MixHue: Color Mixer ("the App") is provided by Logan CHOI ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use MixHue: Color Mixer.

Information We Collect

MixHue: Color Mixer does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

The App lets you create, mix, adjust, compare, and save colors on your device. The App does not maintain user accounts and does not store personal information on our own servers.

Color values, saved colors, and color combinations are processed as part of the App's features and may be stored locally on your device.

Photo and Camera Use

MixHue lets you pick colors from photos or camera captures.

Images selected from your device or captured with the camera are used only to extract colors on your device. We do not upload, store, sell, or share your photos or camera images on our own servers.

Camera captures may be saved temporarily in the App cache only to complete the color picking action. These temporary files may be removed by the operating system, when the App cache is cleared, when App data is cleared, or when the App is uninstalled.

These services may automatically collect or process certain data to provide ads, measure ad performance, analyze App usage, prevent fraud and abuse, improve security, and maintain service reliability.

Depending on your device, settings, region, and use of the App, this data may include:

  • IP address

  • App launches and in-app interactions

Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK, and Firebase Analytics process information according to Google's policies and controls.

European Privacy Choices

In App versions that include European privacy choices, users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked whether they are at least 16 before advertising and analytics are enabled. We store the age category and privacy choices on the device; we do not ask for a date of birth. If you are under 16 or choose not to answer, the App disables advertising and Firebase Analytics.

For users aged 16 or older in these regions, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides applicable consent choices. You can consent, decline, or manage the available purposes and partners. You can revisit your age response and applicable consent choices through Privacy choices in the App's settings.

The App checks whether consent information needs updating at startup. A valid saved choice generally does not require another consent message, but a new message may be necessary when consent information expires, requirements change, or App data is cleared. The European consent message is targeted to the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, rather than to users based on nationality.

Advertising and analytics permissions are handled separately. Declining consent does not necessarily remove all advertising: where Google's platform permits it, limited ads may still be served and involve data processing described by Google. Firebase Analytics collection is disabled when the applicable analytics permission is not granted. Changing consent affects subsequent processing and does not itself delete previously processed data.

These in-app controls are available only in versions that include this feature. Older versions may not offer them; update the App to access the controls when that update becomes available. Outside the targeted regions, this European consent message is not shown and the advertising and analytics practices described above may apply.

Remote App Configuration

The App uses Firebase Remote Config to retrieve settings that control advertising features and their availability. This service may process Firebase installation identifiers and technical information such as country code, language, time zone, operating system and platform versions, App identifiers, and SDK version. When integrated with Analytics, it may also use Analytics user properties and first-open information. These data support retrieving and applying App configuration.

In versions that include European privacy choices, the App requests remote configuration only when its analytics permission check allows it. If settings cannot be retrieved, locally stored or default settings are used.

Advertising ID

The App may use the Android advertising ID through Google Mobile Ads SDK and Firebase Analytics.

The advertising ID may be used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID through your Android device settings. Depending on your region and device settings, you may also be shown privacy choices or consent options related to advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share photos or camera images with our own servers.

However, third-party services used by the App, such as Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK, and Firebase Analytics, may collect, process, or share data as described in their own policies and documentation. This processing may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, service reliability, and related purposes.

Data Retention

We do not maintain user accounts and do not store personal information on our own servers.

Color data, saved colors, and color combinations may remain on your device as App data. You can delete this data by clearing the App's data in your device settings or uninstalling the App.

Images selected from photos or captured with the camera are used for on-device color extraction. Camera captures may remain temporarily in the App cache and may be removed by the operating system, when the App cache is cleared, when App data is cleared, or when the App is uninstalled.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics may retain data according to their own retention policies.

If you have questions about data deletion or believe that we hold information related to you, you may contact us using the contact information below. If we hold any applicable information, we will handle deletion requests in accordance with applicable laws.
