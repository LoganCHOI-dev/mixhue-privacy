Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 20, 2026

MixHue: Color Mixer ("the App") is provided by Logan CHOI ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use MixHue: Color Mixer.

Information We Collect

MixHue: Color Mixer does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information directly.

The App lets you create, mix, adjust, compare, and save colors locally on your device. The App does not maintain user accounts and does not store personal information on our own servers.

Photo and Camera Use

MixHue lets you pick colors from photos or camera captures. Images selected from your device or captured with the camera are used only to extract colors on your device.

We do not upload, store, sell, or share your photos on our own servers. Camera captures may be saved temporarily in the app cache only to complete the color picking action.

Data Retention

MixHue: Color Mixer does not maintain user accounts or store personal information on our own servers.
